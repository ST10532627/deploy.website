About Paws & Hope Animal Rescue

Founded in 2019 by animal lover, Amahle Luthuli, Paws & Hope started with one kennel in Alberton North and a big dream: a community where no animal is left behind.

We are a registered Non-Profit Organisation dedicated to rescuing stray, abandoned and abused animals. Many rescues com from Alberton, Brackenhurst and Thokoza.

Our Mission

To rescue, rehabilitate and rehome every animal in our care with compassion and dignity.

Our Vision

A Gauteng where every pet is loved, and no animal suffers on the streets.
